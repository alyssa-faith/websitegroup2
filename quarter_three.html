QUARTER 3 Gender Inequality Solutions

Quarter 2 was quite easy as we have a bit of a grapple on the basics of coding. We typically focused on gender equality this time—or more so on women's discrimination and rights. We used the RCA or also known as Root Cause Analysis in which we had to use at least two RCA methods—we chose the fish bone diagram and a mind map. One for the analysis and one for the solutions to our problem, which is the discrimination against women. Our final solution wasn't too extra as it was to protest against women discrimination or/and support women rights campaign.

Link: Survey Question

For the analysis part, we analyzed how many respondents chose this and that; most of our respondents think that the poster is an effective way to give awareness about discrimination. They also said that the designs are great and appealing. However, some think that it lacks information, which is one of the things we need to improve.

Link: Survey Analysis

The solution is about campaigning and protesting for women's rights with the help of women empowerment. With this kind of solution, we figured that a poster can be a great way to achieve the solution. The design shows women raising their hand representing the willingness to fight for their rights. It also has texts that convey the message of the poster.
